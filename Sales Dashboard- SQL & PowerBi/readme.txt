📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

📖 Project Overview
The Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, orders, discounts, customer purchasing behavior, and category-wise performance across different countries and store locations.
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business KPIs and enables users to explore sales trends using interactive filters for Country, Store Location, Category, Payment Method, and Date.


🎯 Business Objectives

📈 Monitor overall sales and profitability performance
💰 Analyze total profit and average order value
🛒 Track order volume and discount distribution
🌍 Compare sales performance across countries and store locations
📅 Identify monthly and daily sales trends
💳 Understand customer payment method preferences
🏷️ Analyze category-wise sales performance
📊 Evaluate the relationship between discounts and profit

📂 Dashboard Features

📌 Executive KPI Cards
The dashboard provides high-level business KPIs:
💰 Total Sales: 4.14M
📈 Total Profit: 958.76K
🛒 Total Orders: 3K
🏷️ Total Discount: 76.67K
🧾 Average Order Value: 1.38K


🌍 Sales by Store Locations
An interactive geographical map visualizes sales across different countries and store locations, including:
🇨🇦 Canada
🇨🇳 China
🇮🇳 India
🇳🇬 Nigeria
🇬🇧 UK
🇺🇸 US
This helps identify geographical sales concentration and compare performance across locations.


📈 Monthly Sales Trends
The monthly trend chart tracks sales performance throughout the year and helps identify:
📅 High-performing months
📉 Low-performing periods
📊 Seasonal sales patterns
📈 Overall sales movement
💸 Discounts Given vs Profit
A scatter plot analyzes the relationship between Total Discount and Total Profit across countries and locations.
This helps evaluate whether higher discounts are associated with increased profitability and identify locations with different discount-profit patterns.


💳 Sales by Payment Method
A donut chart shows the contribution of different payment methods:
📱 Mobile Payment
💳 Credit Card
💵 Cash
This provides insights into customer payment preferences and their contribution to overall sales.


📅 Daily Sales Trends
Sales are compared across different days of the week to identify:
🔥 High-performing days
📉 Low-performing days
📊 Weekly sales patterns
🗓️ Potential opportunities for targeted sales strategies


🏆 Sales by Category
The category-wise bar chart compares total sales across product categories such as:
🏠 Home & Kitchen
👕 Clothing
💻 Electronics
⚽ Sports
💄 Beauty
🧸 Toys
This helps identify the categories contributing the most to overall sales.


🎛️ Interactive Filters
Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:
🌍 Country
📍 Store Location
🏷️ Category
💳 Payment Method
📅 Date Range

💡 Key Insights
💰 The dashboard generated 4.14M in total sales with 958.76K in total profit.

📱 Mobile Payment, Credit Card, and Cash contribute significantly to overall sales.

🏷️ The discount-profit analysis provides visibility into the impact of discounting on profitability.
🌍 Geographical analysis highlights differences in sales performance across countries and store locations.
